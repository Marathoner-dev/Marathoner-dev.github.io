Onleave multilingual media

A four-language publication explaining hot springs and bathing with medical evidence

Period
Jun - Oct 2026
Role
Frontend rebuild, CMS, data cleanup, member features
Team
Team project

Why it exists

Visitors to Korea struggle to find hot spring information in their own language. The goal was a magazine, a hot spring directory, community, and events in Korean, English, Japanese, and Chinese.

By the numbers

267
hot springs left after verifying 5,891 public records
4
languages

What I built

  • 01A CMS for non-developers: live split preview, draft autosave, automatic translation into four languages on save
  • 02Verified 5,891 public records down to 267 real hot springs
  • 03Sign-in, likes, bookmarks, community, and events
  • 04Privacy policy in four languages and consent-based analytics

Design decisions

Translate fully or skip

A partial failure would publish half-translated articles. Instead the whole language is skipped and retried.

Row-level security from day one

Supabase row-level security was on from the start, so a frontend mistake can never expose someone else's data.
