Why it exists

Buying a spa ticket usually means a long sign-up and a paper or SMS check at the door. The goal: sign up with just a phone number, pay in five taps, open the ticket in one. Good enough that other sellers start to feel slow.

Design decisions

A modular monolith

A small team cannot run microservices. Spring Modulith splits members, catalog, inventory, orders, payments, tickets, and rewards into modules, with boundaries enforced by tests.

Contract first

Frontend types are generated from the OpenAPI spec, and tests fail when the spec and the code drift apart.

Rewards only after a confirmed payment

Webhooks, reconciliation against the payment gateway, and idempotency keep payment state correct. Raffle entries are issued only after confirmation.

A draw anyone can verify

A hash of the seed is published before the draw and combined with a public beacon. Anyone can rerun the calculation and get the same winners.

Tickets that are hard to forge

QR codes are signed with Ed25519. There is a fallback check by the last four digits of a phone number, and a five-minute undo.
