Why it exists

Picking a date in a group chat turns into an endless "I can't that day." Here you make one calendar, share the link, everyone marks their days, and the overlap is right there.

What I built

  • 01Join from a link, no sign-up
  • 02Password-protected calendars
  • 03Real-time sync of everyone's availability
  • 04Long-press to lock in the date and place
  • 05KakaoTalk sharing with generated preview images

Design decisions

No sign-up

Scheduling is a one-off task, and nobody signs up for one-off tasks. A link and an optional password are enough.
